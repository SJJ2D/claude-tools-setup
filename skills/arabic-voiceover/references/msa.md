# Writing guide: Modern Standard Arabic (MSA) for voiceover

- **Diacritics:** light, only where reading is ambiguous («عُلِم»/«عَلَم»), plus the final case ending where the meaning would change. Full diacritics make the delivery stiff, like a recitation.
- **Sentence length:** short (≤ 15 words). Join with «و» and «ثم» rather than chains of «الذي».
- **Numbers:** on v4/v3 write them as the user wrote them. In a script you write yourself, use the normal written form.
- **Documentary narration:** `[warmly]` or `[softly]` with natural punctuation. Trailers: `[dramatically]` plus pauses «…».
- **Educational for children:** warm, slower, short sentences; `[excited]` only at moments of surprise.
- **Watch for:** «الـ» merging with sun letters (الشمس) is usually correct. Foreign names: use IPA between /…/ on v4/v3.
